I was invited to give a talk at **Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area National Innovation Center**, entitled **Intelligent Evolution and Collaborative Innovation in Robotics and Autonomous Systems**.

This talk explores the development of intelligence in robotics and Autonomous Systems, drawing on my research in dexterous manipulation, tactile perception, motion planning and control, and dual-arm teleoperation. It discusses how multimodal perception, human-robot collaboration, and autonomous decision-making can be integrated. Through representative research examples, the talk traces the progression from human demonstrations and data collection to contact-aware perception, fine manipulation, and shared autonomy. It also examines how coordinated advances in data, algorithms, hardware, and evaluation can support the reliable operation of intelligent systems in real-world environments.
